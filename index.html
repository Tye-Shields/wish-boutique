About

Upstairs on Brant

Wish Boutique is a women's clothing boutique on Brant Street in Burlington. The shop is on the second floor, with parking and the entrance at the back. Shoppers call it a hidden gem, with welcoming, helpful staff and items thoughtfully curated.

  • Wed–Fri, 11 a.m. – 3 p.m.
  • Saturday, 10 a.m. – 1 p.m.
